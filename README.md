## AI E-commerce Platforms

### All-in-One AI E-commerce Solutions

**[Nexscope](https://www.nexscope.ai?co-from=github)** - Ecommerce data and creative platform for online sellers
- **What it does**: Combines marketplace intelligence with online image and video generators and developer APIs
- **Data capabilities**: Product, keyword, pricing, review, ranking, seller, store, sourcing, advertising, and compliance research across supported ecommerce sources
- **Creative capabilities**: Browser-based image and video generation plus documented Creative APIs for models and reusable ecommerce workflows
- **Developer access**: REST APIs and MCP tools for documented data and creative capabilities; supported connection methods and requirements are listed in each tool’s documentation
- **Best for**: Sellers, ecommerce teams, developers, and agencies that need research data and product-content tools in one platform
- **AI capabilities**: Image and video generation, editing, enhancement, virtual try-on, and structured workflows where documented
- **Services**: Also offers [done-for-you ecommerce growth services](https://www.nexscope.ai/ecommerce-growth-services?co-from=github) for sellers who prefer expert help

[Explore MCP tools →](https://www.nexscope.ai/mcp?co-from=github&utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=github_mcp_launch&utm_content=awesome-ai-ecommerce-tools_readme_mcp) · [View API docs →](https://www.nexscope.ai/api-docs?co-from=github&utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=github_mcp_launch&utm_content=awesome-ai-ecommerce-tools_readme_api)

## Pricing & Revenue Optimization

### Dynamic Pricing

**[Prisync](https://prisync.com)** — Monitors competitor prices and optimizes pricing strategies automatically

**[Intelligence Node](https://www.intelligencenode.com)** — Competitive pricing and assortment optimization

**[Aura](https://goaura.com)** - AI-powered Amazon repricing
- **What it does**: Automatically adjusts Amazon listing prices to balance sales velocity and profit margin
- **Key features**: Maven AI strategies, automatic price limits, scheduled workflows, pricing activity logs, bulk listing updates
- **Pricing**: From $451/year with annual billing; 14-day free trial; Starter includes Maven for 250 listings
- **Best for**: Amazon sellers managing competitive listings and repricing across their catalog
- **AI capabilities**: Maven learns from listing and competitor behavior, adjusts pricing strategies, and calculates maximum prices dynamically

### Revenue Optimization

**[Dynamic Yield](https://www.dynamicyield.com)** — Personalization, product recommendations, and experience testing for digital commerce.

## Contributing

We welcome contributions! Please read our [Contributing Guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md) before submitting.

### How to Contribute

1. **Fork** this repository
2. **Add** your tool in the appropriate category
3. **Follow** the format: tool name linked to its official product page, followed by a short factual description
4. **Include** a source-backed use case; add prices only with a dated official source and billing terms
5. **Submit** a pull request

### Tool Selection Criteria

- Explain the tool’s AI capability or supporting role in an AI workflow
- Relevant to e-commerce businesses
- Actively maintained and supported
- Clear value proposition for users
